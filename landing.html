Why processes stay slow

The problems are rarely where people look.

Work waits in inboxes. Nobody quite owns the hand-off. The same data is typed in twice. Everyone feels it, but nobody can show where the time and money actually go.

Hidden waiting

Most of a process's time is spent waiting, not working. It doesn't show up in anyone's to-do list.

Risks nobody owns

Single approvers, shared logins, manual re-keying: small gaps that turn into refunds, complaints and audit findings.

No numbers to act on

Without a cost, a target and an owner, improvement ideas stay ideas. UAIM gives you all three.

Walk through the risks with the people who do the work.

For every step, UAIM sets out what can go wrong, why, how likely it is and how you'd notice. Your team reviews one step at a time, corrects the ratings and adds what only they know.

  • One step on screen at a time, no overwhelming spreadsheet
  • Add your own risks whenever you spot one
  • Every rating can be challenged and changed
Analyse

Find out where the days actually go.

The Process Efficiency Analysis shows working time and waiting time for every step, then calculates lead time and how much of it adds value. Timings are never invented: they come from your description or your team.

  • Lead time, waiting time and value-added share, calculated
  • The biggest wait and the longest step highlighted
  • Clear labels show what's measured and what's assumed

Each recommended move shows which risks it reduces, how much waiting it removes and how to measure it. Mark actions done, re-rate the risks, and the results appear against your starting point.

About us

Work should feel better than this.

Overflowing inboxes, unnecessary hand-offs and small tasks that nobody seems to own can make everyday work harder than it needs to be.

These are real pressures, and the people dealing with them often know exactly what isn't working. UAIM was built to turn that knowledge into meaningful, lasting improvement.

We start with the processes people use every day, which are too rarely reviewed with input from the people who actually work with them. Our approach is clear, practical and grounded in evidence about how work can be improved.

When people help shape a process, it doesn't just become more efficient. They are also more likely to understand it, support it and follow it.

Better working cultures cannot be created by senior managers, middle managers or frontline teams alone. Nor should individuals be left to cope with problems that organisations have the ability to fix.

UAIM puts the people who do the work at the centre of improvement. By actively involving employees in reviewing each stage, sharing their knowledge and challenging what isn't working, we help create a stronger sense of engagement, ownership and shared responsibility. Together, they help shape and sign off changes that make work better for everyone.

Better processes, built for the people who use them.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Which processes can I review?

Any process that repeats: customer refunds, onboarding, purchasing, month-end close, maintenance requests, recruitment, order handling. If you can describe the steps, UAIM can review it.

Do I need to know Lean, SIPOC or risk analysis?

No. The review guides you through each stage in plain language. The methods are there underneath, so the structure is sound, but you never need to learn the jargon.

How long does a review take?

The AI prepares each stage in under a minute. Checking the stages with your team usually takes an afternoon, spread over as many sessions as you like.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It will, sometimes: that's why your team checks every stage. Everything can be corrected, assumptions are labelled, and a review is only validated when your team signs it off.

Is my data safe?

Reviews are visible only to people in your company. The AI service we use processes your text to produce the review and doesn't use it to train its models. Use anonymised descriptions if a process contains personal data.

Is it a subscription?

No. You buy review credits when you need them, and they don't expire. Your first review is free.
